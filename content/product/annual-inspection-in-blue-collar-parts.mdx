**Blue Collar Parts can record an annual inspection on a truck or trailer and send it to your customer like any other record.** The inspection is yours: you attest to your qualification, you perform it, and you are responsible for it. Blue Collar Parts records it; it does not verify, certify, or establish compliance.

## Before you start: your qualification record

The first time you start an annual inspection, Blue Collar Parts asks you to save a qualification record describing how you meet the federal inspector qualifications. Blue Collar Parts does not verify it. Read [how to become a DOT inspector](/kb/who-can-perform-dot-inspection) for what the qualification requires. You can view your qualification record later under **QUALIFICATION** in your [account](/account#qual).

## Record the inspection

1. [Start a service event](/customers?new=event) and choose **Annual inspection** as the **Type of work**. Annual inspections are available for truck and trailer assets only.
2. Work through the inspection categories. The inspection follows the categories of Appendix A to Part 396; see the [Appendix A checklist](/kb/appendix-a-inspection-standards).
3. Press **FILE INSPECTION** and complete the details: the **Inspection** fields (authority, jurisdiction, date, time, and location) and the **Vehicle** fields.
4. Mark the work complete and send the record as you would a PM: see [send a record to your customer](/kb/send-a-record-to-your-customer).

## What the record does not do

- It does not certify the inspector or the vehicle, and it does not establish compliance with 49 CFR Part 396.
- It does not replace the motor carrier's own recordkeeping. Federal rules put retention of the inspection report on the carrier or other responsible entity; see [the annual inspection report form](/kb/annual-inspection-report-form) and [where Blue Collar Parts fits against Part 396](/kb/bcp-and-part-396-recordkeeping).
- It does not issue an inspection decal.
