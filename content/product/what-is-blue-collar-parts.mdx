Blue Collar Parts helps technicians create **digital preventive-maintenance records** for commercial equipment. A technician starts a service event for a customer and asset, records the work performed, and retains a clear history of the visit.

The immediate job is simple: make a service record useful after the technician leaves the equipment. The platform is built for trucks, trailers, generators, and marine equipment, with a preventive-maintenance workflow available today.

## The problem

Preventive maintenance produces more than a checklist. It creates a record of what was checked, what was found, what was done, and when the equipment needs attention again. When that information stays in a technician's notebook, inbox, or one-off PDF, the equipment owner has little usable history to work from.

## What the platform does

For preventive maintenance, the platform supports:

1. **Customer and asset context.** Start with the customer and the particular truck, trailer, generator, or vessel.
2. **Preventive-maintenance service entry.** Record a PM visit as work on that asset rather than as an unconnected form.
3. **Service history.** Keep the service event attached to the asset so the next visit starts with context.

See [what a PM inspection is](/kb/what-is-a-pm-inspection) for the record structure and [what an equipment owner should receive](/kb/what-belongs-in-a-pm-report-for-the-equipment-owner) for the customer-facing outcome.

## What equipment it covers

The current PM workflow covers truck, marine-diesel, and diesel-generation maintenance forms. It is not a substitute for a manufacturer service manual, and it is not an annual DOT inspection.

## What it deliberately does not do

This matters as much as the feature list, and it is stated plainly because ambiguity here is a liability:

- **It does not make a regulatory determination.** A PM result does not certify compliance, replace a federal annual inspection, or create an out-of-service judgment. Read [PM inspection vs. DOT inspection](/kb/pm-inspection-vs-dot-inspection) for the distinction.
- **It does not replace manufacturer guidance.** The manufacturer’s documentation controls equipment-specific service intervals, limits, and procedures.
- **It does not turn a draft into a customer deliverable automatically.** Publication and customer distribution are separate product capabilities and remain in development.
- **No machine output publishes a record.** Where automated processing assists a technician, the technician’s input remains distinguishable and a person retains the approval boundary.

## Availability

The platform is in active development. The preventive-maintenance workflow is available; pages marked **in development** describe planned capabilities that are not yet generally available.
