**Cummins gives the X15's typical oil change capacity, pan and filter, as 36.3 liters (9.6 gal) low and 43.8 liters (11.6 gal) max.** Cummins gives that range for 2020 and 2021 X15 efficiency hardware and for the EPA 2024 X15. The EPA 2017 X15 has its own figures, by oil pan. Confirm them for the specific engine before filling.

Cummins titles its X15 quick reference guides by emissions certification or hardware, not by model year. They give the oil change capacity, pan and filter, as follows:

| X15 | Oil change capacity (pan and filter) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| EPA 2024 | Typical: low 36.3 liters (9.6 gal), max 43.8 liters (11.6 gal) | Cummins Bulletin 6553662, Rev. 11/24 |
| 2020 and 2021 efficiency hardware | Typical: low 36.3 liters (9.6 gal), max 43.8 liters (11.6 gal) | Cummins Bulletin 5600512, Rev. 10/24 |
| EPA 2017, stamped steel oil pan | 43.5 liters (11.5 gal) | Cummins Bulletin 5410641, Rev. 11/17 |
| EPA 2017, aluminum oil pan (wedge type cast) | 45.4 liters (12 gal) | Cummins Bulletin 5410641, Rev. 11/17 |
| EPA 2017, aluminum oil pan (rear center sump) | 41.6 liters (11 gal) | Cummins Bulletin 5410641, Rev. 11/17 |

## How much oil does a Cummins X15 take, by certification?

- **EPA 2024:** *Quick Reference Guide: Truck maintenance and operation, X15 (EPA 2024)* (Bulletin 6553662, Rev. 11/24), at [mart.cummins.com](https://mart.cummins.com/imagelibrary/data/assetfiles/0077777.pdf), lists "Typical oil change capacity (pan and filter)" as "Low 36.3 liters (9.6 gal)" and "Max 43.8 liters (11.6 gal)". It refers to the X15 CM2450 X124B Owner's Manual, bulletin 5504581.
- **2020 and 2021 efficiency hardware:** *Quick Reference Guide: Truck maintenance and operation, 2020 and 2021 X15 efficiency hardware* (Bulletin 5600512, Rev. 10/24), at [mart.cummins.com](https://mart.cummins.com/imagelibrary/data/assetfiles/0064269.pdf), gives the same "Typical oil change capacity (pan and filter)", and in its maintenance notes: "Typical oil change volume: 36.3–43.8 liters (9.6–11.6 gal)."
- **EPA 2017:** *Truck Maintenance and Operation, X15 (EPA 2017), Quick Reference Guide* (Bulletin 5410641, Rev. 11/17), at [mart.cummins.com](https://mart.cummins.com/imagelibrary/data/assetfiles/0032808.pdf), gives the "Oil Change Capacity (oil pan and filter filled to capacity)" by pan, as in the table. It also gives the "Total Lubricating Oil System Capacity Including Filter and Residual" as 49.2 liters (13 gal) for stamped steel and 43.5 liters (11.5 gal) for aluminum.

For a 2025 or 2026 truck, find which certification the engine's documentation gives, then use that guide. An X15 of a certification or build not listed here has its own documentation.

## How often is the oil changed on an X15?

The 2020 and 2021 guide (Bulletin 5600512) sets the oil drain interval by duty cycle, measured in fuel economy:

- Severe (3–4.9 mpg): 25,000 miles / 12 months
- Short haul (5–5.9 mpg): 50,000 miles / 12 months
- Normal (6–6.9 mpg): 60,000 miles / 12 months
- Light (7+ mpg): 75,000 miles / 12 months

Across the same row it gives "500 hours for vocational applications." It also attaches other conditions to these figures. An extreme severe duty cycle (under 3 mpg) has a shorter interval, and some extensions depend on the oil, the filter or Cummins' OilGuard program. Read those notes in the guide for the engine before setting an interval.

## When is the X15 overhead adjusted?

The 2020 and 2021 guide lists **overhead adjustment** at "500,000 miles / 10,000 hours / 60 months". It gives no clearance value. The adjustment procedure and settings are in Cummins' service documentation for the engine.

## Confirm it for your engine

For full maintenance detail, the 2020 and 2021 guide and the EPA 2024 guide both refer to the *X15 CM2450 X124B Owner's Manual* (Bulletin 5504581) and *Operation and Maintenance Manual* (Bulletin 5504582). Both are available through [Cummins QuickServe](https://quickserve.cummins.com) or a Cummins dealer. Confirm the capacity and intervals for the engine's serial number and build there.

## What should the service record show?

An X15 oil service record should show:

- the date and the mileage or hours;
- the oil grade and the quantity added;
- the filter part numbers;
- the duty cycle the interval was based on;
- the condition of the oil and filter that came out;
- any oil analysis result from the visit (see [what a diesel oil analysis tells you](/kb/diesel-engine-pm-heavy-trucks)).

## Log the oil service in Blue Collar Parts

The truck PM form's **Engine Oil & Filter** section records the service and what was found, and the finished visit becomes the record your customer receives.
