**Volvo's body builder instructions give the D13 oil capacity as 33 quarts (31 L), and Mack's give the MP7 and MP8 oil capacity as 44 quarts (42 L).** Both documents also give a separate *oil change volume*, which depends on the oil pan and the number of full-flow filters on the truck. That is the figure to check against when changing the oil.

## What is the Volvo D13 oil capacity?

**Volvo gives the D13 oil capacity as 33 quarts (31 L).** The source is Volvo's *Body Builder Instructions, VNL, Section 1* (USA174188411, dated 5.2024), published at [volvotrucks.us](https://www.volvotrucks.us/media/vtna/files/shared/body-builder/manuals/2025/section-1.pdf), which lists the D13 "Oil Capacity in quarts" as "33 (31 L)".

For a D13 on a vehicle with two full-flow oil filters, the same section gives the oil change volume by oil pan:

| D13, two full-flow oil filters | Oil change volume | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Steel oil pan | 37 qt (35 L) | Volvo USA174188411 (5.2024) |
| Composite oil pan | 37 qt (35 L) | Volvo USA174188411 (5.2024) |
| Aluminum oil pan | 38 qt (36 L) | Volvo USA174188411 (5.2024) |

The same table also gives an "Oil pan volume" of 33 (31 L) for the steel and composite pans and 34 (32 L) for the aluminum pan. The document does not define "oil capacity", "oil pan volume" or "oil change volume".

## How much oil does a Mack MP8 take?

**Mack gives the MP7 and MP8 oil capacity as 44 quarts (42 L).** The source is Mack's *Body Builder Instructions* (USA189962715, dated 8.2025), published at [macktrucks.com](https://www.macktrucks.com/media/documents/mack-hd-section1.pdf), which lists the "Oil Capacity" for "MP7 and MP8" as "44 quarts (42 L)".

At an oil change, the amount depends on the oil pan and on whether the truck has two or three full-flow oil filters. The same document gives the MP8 figures for each:

| MP8 | Oil change volume | Total volume of oil (dry engine) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Three full-flow filters, steel oil pan | 39 qt (37 L) | 43.8 qt (41.5 L) | Mack USA189962715 (8.2025) |
| Three full-flow filters, composite oil pan | 34.9 qt (33 L) | 39.6 qt (37.5 L) | Mack USA189962715 (8.2025) |
| Three full-flow filters, aluminum oil pan | 40.2 qt (38 L) | 45.4 qt (43 L) | Mack USA189962715 (8.2025) |
| Two full-flow filters, steel oil pan | 36.9 qt (35 L) | 41.7 qt (39.5 L) | Mack USA189962715 (8.2025) |
| Two full-flow filters, composite oil pan | 36.9 qt (35 L) | 41.7 qt (39.5 L) | Mack USA189962715 (8.2025) |
| Two full-flow filters, aluminum oil pan | 38 qt (36 L) | 43.3 qt (41 L) | Mack USA189962715 (8.2025) |

The document gives the dry-engine total separately from the oil change volume, and also lists the filter volume, the pan volume and the pre-fill for each row.

## How often is the oil changed on a D13 or MP8?

Volvo and Mack set the oil and filter change interval by duty cycle, measured by fuel economy, and by the oil used. Both publish it on a *Service at a Glance* maintenance-interval sheet for trucks manufactured in 2022 (GHG) and newer:

- **Volvo D11/D13:** [Service at a Glance Maintenance Intervals, VNL, VNR, VNX, VHD and VAH, Manufactured Year 2022 GHG and Newer](https://www.volvotrucks.us/media/vtna/files/saag-2022-ghg-newer-poster-011023-eng.pdf) (PV960-V-SaaG-011023). For normal duty (greater than 6 mpg), it gives 60,000 miles with Volvo Premium Motor Oil VDS-5 and 55,000 miles with other Volvo-approved VDS-5 oil.
- **Mack MP7/MP8 in Pinnacle, Granite, TerraPro and Anthem trucks:** [Service at a Glance Maintenance Intervals for Pinnacle, Granite, TerraPro and Anthem, Manufactured Year 2022 GHG and Newer](https://www.macktrucks.com/media/files/parts-and-service/support/mack-serviceataglance-pv960-m-saag-22-121522.pdf) (PV960-M-SAAG-22-121522). For normal duty (greater than 6 mpg), it gives 60,000 miles with Mack Engine Oil EOS-5 and 55,000 miles with other Mack-approved EOS-5 oil.

Both sheets give separate miles, kilometres and hours for economy, heavy and severe duty, apply "whichever occurs first", and say: "If idle time is greater than 30%, use the next lower drain interval." Both also note: "VAH low capacity oil pan oil drain interval is 20,000 miles. Use severe duty category for all filters." They call the intervals general recommendations and point to the vehicle's maintenance and lubrication manual. The sheets cover trucks manufactured in 2022 and newer; an older truck follows its own maintenance schedule. Mack's body builder instructions also say: "Refer to the Owners Manual for Oil and Filter change intervals."

## Why the capacity and the oil change volume differ

Both documents state capacity and oil change volume as separate figures, and neither defines the terms. The oil change volume is what the documents give for the pan and filter set-up on a given truck, so a technician needs to know which pan and how many full-flow filters the truck has before filling. Body builder instructions are written for truck builders. Confirm the figures against Volvo's or Mack's service documentation for the engine's serial number and build, or with a dealer.

## What should the service record show?

A D13 or MP8 oil service record should show:

- the oil pan material and the number of full-flow filters;
- the date and the mileage or hours;
- the oil grade and the quantity added;
- the filter part numbers;
- the condition of the oil and filters that came out.
