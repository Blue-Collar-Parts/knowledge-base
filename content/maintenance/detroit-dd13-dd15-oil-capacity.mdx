**Detroit's published documentation gives a service fill (oil and filter change) of 45 qt (43 L) for the DD15 Gen 5 and 40 qt (38 L) for the Gen 5 DD13 Truck.** The DD15 spec sheet also gives the valve lash adjustment interval by duty. It gives an interval, not a lash setting.

## What is the DD15 oil capacity?

Detroit Diesel's *DD15 Gen 5 Technical Specifications* sheet (DDC-EMC-FLY-0121-1121), published through [Daimler Truck North America](https://dtnacontent-dtna.prd.freightliner.com/content/dam/enterprise/documents/DDCPWR%2015904%20-%20DD15%20Gen%205%20Engine%20Vocational%20Spec%20Sheet_3.0.pdf), states "Service Fill (Oil & Filter Change) . . . 45 qt (43 L)".

The same sheet's maintenance table gives the engine oil and filter change interval by duty:

- Severe Duty: 35,000 miles (56,000 km) or 750 hours
- Short-Haul: 45,000 miles (72,000 km) or 1,000 hours
- Long-Haul: 60,000 miles (97,000 km)
- Efficient Long-Haul: 75,000 miles (121,000 km)

The sheet states that these intervals assume Detroit Fluids Specification 93K222 (CK-4) or 93K223 (FA-4) approved oils. A second footnote on the oil and filter change row says the intervals are "Recommended based on using Detroit's lube oil and oil analysis program." The sheet also defines each duty by annual mileage and fuel economy in its footnotes. Read those definitions on the sheet before choosing an interval.

## When is DD15 valve lash adjusted?

The DD15 sheet lists **valve lash adjustment** as:

- Severe Duty: "Adjust every 200,000" miles (322,000 km)
- Short-Haul, Long-Haul and Efficient Long-Haul: "Adjust every 500,000" miles (800,000 km)

The sheet gives no clearance value. The settings and the adjustment procedure are in Detroit's service documentation for the engine.

## What is the DD13 oil capacity?

Detroit's *DD Platform Gen 5 Operator Manual*, on Daimler Truck North America's [public technical literature site](https://www.dtnatechlit.com/portal-public/0000009834-dd-platform-gen-5-operator-manual/0000009834.xml/$/0000009649), gives the DD13 Truck "Service Fill (Oil and Filter Change)" as 38.0 L (40.0 qt). The same table, "Engine Oil Capacities - DD13", gives the oil pan capacity as 35.0 L (37.0 qt) at the high limit and 30.0 L (32.0 qt) at the low limit.

Detroit Diesel's *DD13 Technical Specifications* sheet (2024) for the Australian market, published by [Freightliner Australia](https://www.freightliner.com.au/siteassets/documents/detroit-spec-sheet-dd13-2024.pdf), states the same service fill: "Service Fill (Oil & Filter Change)... 40 qt. (38 L)".

## Confirm it for your engine

The DD15 figure comes from a specification sheet. The DD13 figure comes from the operator manual and, for the Australian market, a specification sheet. None is the service manual. Confirm the capacity, the interval and the valve settings for the engine's serial number and build against Detroit's service documentation, through [Demand Detroit](https://demanddetroit.com) or a Detroit dealer.

## What should the service record show?

A DD13 or DD15 oil service record should show:

- the date and the mileage or hours;
- the oil grade and specification, and the quantity added;
- the filter part numbers;
- the duty the interval was based on;
- the condition of the oil and filter that came out.

A valve lash adjustment is its own line of work in the record, with the mileage at which it was done, because it runs on a different interval from the oil service.

## Log the work in Blue Collar Parts

The truck PM form's **Engine Oil & Filter** section records the oil service, and the finished visit becomes the record your customer receives.
