**PACCAR gives the MX-13 oil system capacity as 42 U.S. quarts, on its spec sheets for both the EPA 2021 and the EPA 2024 engine.** The sheets do not break that figure down by oil pan. Confirm the capacity for the specific engine in the MX engine operator's manual before filling.

## How much oil does a PACCAR MX-13 take?

PACCAR's MX-13 specification sheets give one figure, the oil system capacity, and give the same figure for the EPA 2021 and EPA 2024 engines:

| MX-13 certification | Oil system capacity | Source |
|---|---|---|
| EPA 2021 | 42 U.S. qt | PACCAR MX-13 spec sheet, form 2021MX13BSPEC |
| EPA 2024 | 42 U.S. qt | PACCAR MX-13 spec sheet, form 2024MX13SPEC |
| CARB 2024 / EPA 2024 | 42 U.S. qt | PACCAR MX-13 spec sheet, form 2024MXCARBSPEC |

The sheets are published on peterbilt.com: [EPA 2021](https://www.peterbilt.com/static-assets/documents/resources/pac56_mx13specsheet_2021_final_digitalhires.pdf), [EPA 2024](https://www.peterbilt.com/static-assets/documents/resources/EPA-MX-13-Spec-Sheet_2024_English.pdf) and [CARB 2024 / EPA 2024](https://www.peterbilt.com/static-assets/documents/resources/CARB-MX-13-Spec-Sheet_10222024_English.pdf). Each lists "OIL SYSTEM CAPACITY 42 U.S. QTS." None gives an oil change volume, a figure by oil pan, or a figure for a dry engine, so the amount added at an oil change comes from the MX engine operator's manual. An MX-13 certified to another emissions year has its own documentation.

## How often is the oil changed on an MX-13?

The EPA 2024 sheet gives the oil and filter interval in miles (and kilometers) by duty:

- Normal/Line Haul, under 20% idle time: 75,000 (120,000 km)
- Normal/Line Haul, over 20% idle time: 50,000 (80,000 km)
- Severe/Vocational Duty: 30,000 (48,000 km)

Idle time is part of how the sheet picks the interval, so a truck that idles a lot falls into a shorter one even on line haul. The sheet says to "Reference MX Engine Operators Manual for engine maintenance requirements."

## Confirm it for your engine

Confirm the capacity, the oil specification and the interval for the engine's serial number and build in the MX engine operator's manual, or with a PACCAR engine dealer.

## What should the service record show?

An MX-13 oil service record should show:

- the date and the mileage or hours;
- the duty and idle-time category the interval was based on;
- the oil grade and the quantity added;
- the filter part number;
- the condition of the oil and filter that came out.

## Log the oil service in Blue Collar Parts

The truck PM form's **Engine Oil & Filter** section records the service and what was found, and the finished visit becomes the record your customer receives.
